Hyrox and ATHX are the two hybrid fitness competitions on the rise. They attract the same crowd, but they test **completely different things**. Here's how to tell them apart.

## The format

- **Hyrox**: a fixed format, identical everywhere in the world. **8 × 1 km runs**, each followed by a workout station (SkiErg, sled, burpees, rower, lunges…). You do it all back to back, with no break, to the finish.
- **ATHX**: about **2h30 split into 6 zones**. You go through a strength zone, an endurance zone and a final metcon, separated by refuelling and recovery zones. See [What is ATHX Games?](/blog/what-is-athx-games).

## The role of strength

This is the biggest difference.

- At **Hyrox**, loads are fixed by category. Being very strong helps a bit, but cardio decides the ranking.
- At **ATHX**, there's a real **Strength zone** where you go for your max load (in 2027: 1RM shoulder-to-overhead, 2RM back squat, 3RM deadlift). Lifting heavy earns points directly. See [the ATHX 2027 Strength zone explained](/athx-2027-workouts/strength-zone).

## Managing fatigue

- **Hyrox** is a continuous effort: everyone arrives tired at every station, and pacing is key.
- **ATHX** includes a **Refuel zone** (10 min) and a **Recovery zone** (30 min). Each quality is tested in better conditions, and the most complete athlete wins.

## Which one should you choose?

| You're more of a… | Choose |
| --- | --- |
| Runner, comfortable with long, steady efforts | Hyrox |
| Strength athlete, from the weight room or CrossFit | ATHX |
| All-rounder who likes varied efforts | ATHX |
| Someone who wants an identical format to compare times | Hyrox |

Nothing stops you doing both! The foundation is the same: a good cardio engine and strong legs. But for ATHX, you also need **real heavy strength work**, often neglected by athletes coming from Hyrox or running. See [preparing for ATHX as a runner](/blog/athx-for-runners).

"Can I wear my belt? What about my straps?" It's one of the first questions before an ATHX. Here's what the **official 2027 standards** say, and the list of what to put in your bag so you don't forget anything.

> The rules change from season to season. Reread the official *movement standards* for your season on the ATHX Games website before the competition: they are the reference.

## Allowed or banned: the table

| Equipment | Strength zone | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lifting belt | Allowed | Banned for the sandbag squats |
| Neoprene knee sleeves | Allowed | One pair only, not rolled |
| Elbow sleeves | Allowed | |
| Wrist wraps | Allowed | |
| Weightlifting shoes | Allowed | |
| Chalk | Allowed | Bring your own |
| Straps | Banned | |
| Hand grips, grip tape, gloves | Banned | |
| Knee or elbow wraps | Banned | |
| Squat suit or briefs | Banned | |

In every zone, **shoes must be worn at all times**.

## The lifting belt

It's allowed for the barbell lifts of the [Strength zone](/athx-2027-workouts/strength-zone): back squat, deadlift and shoulder-to-overhead. If you train with one, keep it. If you've never used one, **don't try it for the first time on race day**: a belt takes practice, especially breathing and positioning.

Note that it's **not allowed for the sandbag squats** in Metcon X. So also train that movement without a belt: see [sandbag technique](/blog/sandbag-technique).

## Grip: chalk yes, straps no

On the [deadlift](/athx-movements/deadlift), no straps: your grip holds the bar. Only **chalk** is allowed, and **you must bring your own**. If your grip gives out before your back, work on the mixed grip or hook grip now, and do your heavy sets without straps during your preparation.

## Knee sleeves and wrist wraps

- **Neoprene knee sleeves**: one pair, worn normally. Rolling them for extra thickness is banned, as are elastic knee wraps.
- **Wrist wraps**: allowed, useful on the [shoulder-to-overhead](/athx-movements/shoulder-to-overhead) when the bar is heavy.
- **Elbow sleeves**: allowed. Elbow wraps are not.

## Shoes

Two options:

1. **One versatile pair**, stable and flexible enough to run in: the simplest solution for the whole race.
2. **Weightlifting shoes for strength**, then a pair of running or training shoes for endurance and Metcon X. If you switch, do it during the Refuel zone, and check on site that it's possible.

Either way, compete in shoes **you've already tested** in training, on long sessions.

## The bag list

- Your shoes, and the second pair if needed
- Belt, knee sleeves, wrist wraps
- **Chalk**
- Two outfits: one for the competition, a dry one for afterwards
- A warm layer for the warm-up and the recovery zones
- Your food and bottles: see [what to eat in the Refuel zone](/blog/what-to-eat-refuel-zone)
- Your written plan: strength attempts, paces, Metcon split if you're in a pair
- ID and registration confirmation

Pack your bag **the day before**, not in the morning. The full run-down of the final days is in [the week before ATHX](/blog/athx-race-week).

## And in training?

Train **with exactly your race-day equipment**: same belt, same shoes, no straps on heavy pulls. For a sample week using the competition movements, [try the free ATHX program](/free-athx-training-week).

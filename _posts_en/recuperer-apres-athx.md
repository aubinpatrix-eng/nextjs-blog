You crossed the line: well done. After 2h30 of strength, running, SkiErg and Metcon X, your body needs time. Recovering well is also preparing what comes next: the next event, or the next cycle. Here's the **7-day** plan.

## On the day

- **Drink**: water, and a drink with sodium if you sweated a lot.
- **Eat within 1 to 2 hours**: carbs to refuel, protein to repair. A real meal is better than a bar.
- **Walk** for a few minutes instead of sitting straight down: recovery is gentler.
- **Change** quickly into dry, warm clothes.
- **Write down how you felt** while it's fresh, before you forget: what worked, what didn't.

## Days 1 and 2: let your body do the work

Soreness is often worst on the second day. That's normal.

- **Sleep**: go to bed early, take a nap if you can.
- **Walking**: 30 to 45 minutes at an easy pace, to get the blood flowing.
- **Gentle mobility**: hips, back, shoulders. No forced stretching on very sore muscles.
- **Food**: protein at every meal, fruit and vegetables, and water throughout the day.

## Days 3 and 4: move without forcing

- **Light cardio**: 20 to 30 minutes of cycling, rowing or very easy running. You should be able to talk.
- **Light technique**: a few sets with an empty bar or very light loads, to get the feel back.

## Days 5 to 7: gradual return

- **Strength** at about 50-60% of your usual loads, with few sets.
- **Endurance** at reduced volume, with no hard sessions.
- If you feel good, get back to normal training **the following week**.

| Days | Priority | Activity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Race day | Refuel | Drink, eat, walk |
| Days 1, 2 | Recover | Sleep, walking, mobility |
| Days 3, 4 | Move | Light cardio, empty-bar technique |
| Days 5 to 7 | Resume | Strength and endurance at reduced volume |

## Warning signs

General soreness is normal. However, **localised pain, swelling, or pain that gets worse** instead of better after a few days should be checked by a health professional before you train again.

## Analysing your race

This is the most useful moment for progress. Once the results are out:

1. **Compare your places in the three zones.** The ranking adds up your places: your weakest zone is the one costing you the most. See where you stand with the [ATHX 2026 results by level](/athx-results).
2. **Reread your notes**: warm-up, attempts, paces, refuelling, transitions.
3. **Choose one priority** for the next cycle: strength, engine, or linking the two.

After Hyrox, **DEKA** is the other big hybrid fitness format often compared with ATHX. Run by Spartan, it also takes place indoors, with machines and loads. But the logic is very different. Here's what you need to know before choosing.

## DEKA in brief: 10 zones and one clock

DEKA offers **three formats**, with the same 10 functional zones:

- **DEKA FIT**: a 500 m run before each zone, 5 km in total;
- **DEKA MILE**: about 160 m of running before each zone, 1 mile in total;
- **DEKA STRONG**: the 10 zones, without running.

The zones come in this order: reverse lunges with a RAM, 500 m row, box jump or step-over, medicine ball sit-up throws, SkiErg, farmer's carry, air bike, dead ball over an obstacle, sled push and pull, and RAM burpees.

Everything is done **on a single clock**, with no breaks. Loads are **fixed** by category: the ranking comes down to your final time.

## ATHX in brief: separate, scored zones

ATHX lasts about 2h30 and is split into **6 zones**, of which only three count towards the ranking:

1. the **Strength zone**: 1RM shoulder-to-overhead, 2RM back squat and 3RM deadlift;
2. the **Endurance zone**: a 3 km run (in ATHX and Pro), then as many SkiErg metres as possible within a 24-minute cap;
3. **Metcon X**: rower, dumbbells, sandbag squats and carry, burpees, with a 25-minute cap.

In between, there are warm-up, refuelling and recovery zones. All the details in our guide to [the ATHX 2027 workouts](/athx-2027-workouts).

## The differences that matter

| | DEKA FIT | ATHX |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Format | 10 linked zones, one clock | 6 zones, 3 of them scored |
| Running | 5 km split into 10 × 500 m | 3 km in one go (ATHX and Pro) |
| Loads | Fixed by category | Maximal in the Strength zone, fixed elsewhere |
| Recovery | None | Refuel zone and Recovery zone |
| Score | Total time | Loads, distance and time depending on the zone |
| No-running formats | DEKA STRONG | No |

### Maximal strength

This is the real dividing line. At DEKA, no zone asks for your max: you're given a load and have to move it fast. At ATHX, the **Strength zone** measures your maximal loads, and **every kilo lifted** counts. A strength athlete scores far more points there. Estimate your loads with the [1RM calculator](/1rm-calculator).

### Running

DEKA FIT splits 5 km into ten 500 m chunks between stations: you often run while recovering from the previous zone. ATHX asks for **3 km in one go**, followed by the SkiErg, within a 24-minute cap. To see how much SkiErg time your running pace leaves you, use the [Endurance zone calculator](/athx-endurance-zone-calculator).

### Managing effort

At DEKA, everything comes down to one continuous effort: you need to pace yourself from start to finish. At ATHX, the recovery zones let you **start almost fresh** on each scored event. The difficulty lies elsewhere: producing a maximal strength effort, then moving on to endurance with heavy legs. Read [managing the strength → endurance transition](/blog/strength-to-endurance-transition).

## Which one should you choose?

- **You like going fast and linking efforts without stopping**: DEKA, especially DEKA FIT, will suit you.
- **You're comfortable under a barbell and an all-rounder**: ATHX will reward your strength, which counts for a third of the ranking.
- **You're a beginner**: DEKA STRONG and the ATHX Lite category are two good entry points. See [which ATHX category to choose](/blog/choose-athx-category).

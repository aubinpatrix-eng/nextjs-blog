After a first French season in 2026 (Paris in April, Marseille in September), ATHX returns in 2027 with **three events in France**. Here's the calendar and the right time to start your training.

## The 2027 ATHX calendar in France

| City | Dates | Venue | Start training (12 weeks) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **[ATHX Paris](/athx-paris-training)** | 13-14 February 2027 | Paris Event Center | mid-November 2026 |
| **[ATHX Montpellier](/athx-montpellier-training)** | 28-29 May 2027 | Parc des Expositions & Sud de France Arena | early March 2027 |
| **[ATHX Marseille](/athx-marseille-training)** | 10-11 September 2027 | Parc Chanot | mid-June 2027 |

Each event offers all three categories (**Lite, ATHX, Pro**), solo or in pairs (men, women, mixed). Spots go fast: register early on the official ATHX website, which is the reference for dates and times.

> Dates checked in September 2026. They may change: check the official website before booking your travel.

## When to start training?

Allow **12 weeks** before the competition for a full preparation:

- **Weeks 1 to 4**: build the base (strength and aerobic endurance);
- **Weeks 5 to 8**: increase loads and link efforts;
- **Weeks 9 to 11**: maximum intensity and Metcon X simulations;
- **Week 12**: deload to arrive fresh.

Before you start, check your loads with our [free 1RM calculator](/1rm-calculator).

## How much does ATHX cost?

As a guide, registration for ATHX Paris was around **€110 solo** and **€220 for a pair**. The price varies with the event, the category and when you register: the first spots often sell at a lower price. The official ATHX Games website is the reference.

Add travel to your budget, accommodation if you're coming from far away, and your training. To train without spending anything, start with our [free training week](/free-athx-training-week). In a pair? Also read [training for ATHX as a duo](/blog/athx-doubles-training).

## Planning your trip

- **Book early** for a hotel near the venue: the events attract thousands of athletes and spectators.
- **Arrive the day before** if you're travelling far: a long journey on race morning hurts performance.
- **Plan your refuelling**: read [what to eat the day before and the morning of ATHX](/blog/what-to-eat-before-athx) and [what to eat in the Refuel zone](/blog/what-to-eat-refuel-zone).

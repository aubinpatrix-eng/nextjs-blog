ATHX offers three categories: **Lite**, **ATHX** and **Pro**. The format and zones stay the same, but the loads and standards go up. Choosing the right category is the first important decision of your preparation.

## Questions to ask yourself

- **Strength**: are you comfortable with the loads set for the category, over several clean reps? Check the official rules for your edition to find them.
- **Endurance**: can you hold a continuous one-hour effort at a steady pace, alternating running and machines?
- **Experience**: have you already taken part in a fitness competition or an obstacle race?

## Our guidelines

- **Lite**: ideal for a first participation, if you've trained regularly for less than two years or if one of your qualities (strength or endurance) is still lagging.
- **ATHX**: for regular athletes, comfortable with both strength and cardio, who already have some competition experience.
- **Pro**: for experienced athletes aiming for the leaderboard, whose competition loads are a moderate fraction of their PRs.

The loads and reps for each category are detailed on our [ATHX Lite](/athx-categories/athx-lite), [ATHX](/athx-categories/athx) and [ATHX Pro](/athx-categories/athx-pro) pages.

## The trap to avoid

Aiming too high the first time is the most common mistake. A competition you enjoy in Lite will teach you far more than one you suffer through in Pro. You can always move up a category at the next edition. For a first competition, targeting the end-of-May event gives you the whole winter to build your base: see our [ATHX Montpellier training](/athx-montpellier-training).

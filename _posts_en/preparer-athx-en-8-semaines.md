You noticed the ATHX date a bit late, or life got in the way of your preparation? Don't panic. Twelve weeks is ideal, but **eight weeks are enough** to be ready, as long as you make choices. Here's the plan.

## Eight weeks: what changes

With less time, you no longer try to develop everything. You keep the essentials:

- **Keep**: heavy strength on the three scored lifts, the run-then-SkiErg combination, Metcon X simulations, the final deload.
- **Cut back**: accessory exercises, running "for fun", sessions that look nothing like the format.
- **Don't**: double your volume to make up for lost time. That's the recipe for injury.

## When to start?

An 8-week preparation starts **on the Monday, 8 weeks before competition week**:

| Event | Competition | Start of the 8 weeks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [ATHX Paris](/athx-paris-training) | 13 and 14 February 2027 | Monday 21 December 2026 |
| [ATHX Montpellier](/athx-montpellier-training) | 28 and 29 May 2027 | Monday 5 April 2027 |
| [ATHX Marseille](/athx-marseille-training) | 10 and 11 September 2027 | Monday 19 July 2027 |

For Paris, the first two weeks fall over the holidays: keep at least one heavy strength session and one endurance session a week, and don't try to make up for it in January.

## The 8-week plan

| Weeks | Goal | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Test and launch | PR tests (or estimates), easy endurance, SkiErg technique |
| 2 to 4 | Build | Strength at 75-85%, one threshold session, one run-then-SkiErg session |
| 5 to 6 | Specific | Strength at 85-92%, strength-then-run combinations, one Metcon X simulation |
| 7 | Sharpen | Attempts close to competition loads, last short simulation |
| 8 | Deload and perform | Volume halved, intensity kept, competition |

### Week 1: know your numbers

Everything starts from your maxes. If you have a recent PR, use it. Otherwise, estimate it from a set of 3 to 5 reps with the [1RM calculator](/1rm-calculator), safer than a real test. See [testing your PRs without getting injured](/blog/test-your-prs-safely).

### Weeks 2 to 4: build the base

Three strength sessions a week on the [Strength zone](/athx-2027-workouts/strength-zone) lifts: shoulder-to-overhead, back squat, deadlift. Alongside, two endurance sessions, including one that reproduces the [Endurance zone](/athx-2027-workouts/endurance-zone): run, then straight onto the SkiErg.

### Weeks 5 and 6: get closer to the format

Loads rise towards 85-92%. Add **pre-fatigue combinations** (heavy squats then a 3 km run) and a **Metcon X simulation** to find your rhythm. That's the part I neglected at my first ATHX: I talk about it in [my Paris race report](/blog/athx-paris-2026-race-report).

### Week 7: sharpen

Go up one last time towards your competition loads, on low reps, to confirm your **opening attempts**. A last short simulation, without emptying the tank.

### Week 8: deload

Halve your volume, keep a little intensity to stay sharp, sleep, and plan your refuelling: see [the week before ATHX, day by day](/blog/athx-race-week) and [what to eat the day before and the morning of ATHX](/blog/what-to-eat-before-athx).

## A sample week

- **3 strength sessions**: each built around one Strength zone lift, with short accessory work.
- **2 endurance sessions**: one threshold or interval session, one linking running and the SkiErg.
- **1 mixed session**: strength-then-run combination, or a Metcon X simulation.

That's 5 to 6 sessions. If you only have 4, merge the mixed session with a strength session. To spread strength and running across the week, read [combining strength training and running without losing strength](/blog/combine-strength-and-running).

## 4 weeks or less left?

- **4 weeks**: stop trying to improve, learn the format. Week 1: estimate your PRs and one session for each zone. Weeks 2 and 3: combinations and one simulation. Week 4: deload.
- **2 weeks**: a light simulation of each zone in the first week, then active rest. You'll gain more places by knowing the format than with two weeks of grinding.
- **In every case**: choose a realistic category. See [Lite, ATHX or Pro: which category to choose](/blog/choose-athx-category).

## Where to start this week?

The simplest way: do **week 1 of the program** right now. [Try the free ATHX program](/free-athx-training-week): 5 sessions based on percentages of your PRs, with strength, SkiErg, rower and a Metcon. The [full ATHX PREP program](/#cta) then takes over, with loads calculated from your PRs.

Twelve weeks of preparation, and everything can still be decided in the last seven days. Too much training, and you arrive tired. Not enough, and your legs feel heavy. Here's how to handle **the last week before ATHX**, day by day.

## The principle: less volume, a little intensity

Fitness is built during your preparation. The last week is for **letting it show**: you recover from accumulated fatigue without losing what you've gained.

- **Volume**: roughly halved (fewer sets, fewer kilometres).
- **Intensity**: kept on a few short sets, to keep the feel of load and speed.
- **New things**: none. No new exercise, no new shoes, no new supplement.

## The day-by-day plan

| Day | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Day -7 | Usual strength session, volume cut by half |
| Day -6 | Easy 30-min run + a few strides |
| Day -5 | **Last heavy session**: singles at your opening attempt loads |
| Day -4 | Easy SkiErg or rower, with a few bursts at race pace |
| Day -3 | Rest or mobility |
| Day -2 | Short run at race pace (1 to 2 km), then rest |
| Day -1 | 20-min activation at most, pack your bag |
| Race day | Competition |

At day -5, don't chase a record: one or two clean reps **at your opening load** are enough to confirm your attempts. Calculate them with the [1RM calculator](/1rm-calculator).

## Sleep: your best weapon

The night before the race is often restless with nerves. That's fine: **it's the nights of the whole week that count**. Aim for 8 hours a night from day -7, keep regular hours, and switch off screens in the evening.

## Food

- **Keep your habits**: the week before is not the time to change your diet.
- **Increase carbs a little** over the last two days: pasta, rice, potatoes, bread.
- **Hydrate** regularly, without overdoing it the evening before.

Meal details are in [what to eat the day before and the morning of ATHX](/blog/what-to-eat-before-athx), and refuelling during the race in [what to eat in the Refuel zone](/blog/what-to-eat-refuel-zone).

## The written plan

Before you leave, write down on a sheet of paper or in your phone:

1. **Your attempts** in the Strength zone, with the opener and the load jumps.
2. **Your warm-up** in order, with a plan B if the racks aren't available: see [warming up before a 1RM](/blog/warm-up-before-1rm).
3. **Your paces** for the run and the SkiErg, calculated with the [Endurance zone calculator](/athx-endurance-zone-calculator).
4. **Your Metcon X strategy**, and the split if you're in a pair: see [training for ATHX as a pair](/blog/athx-doubles-training).
5. **Your refuelling** for the Refuel zone.

On race day, there's nothing left to decide: just follow the plan.

## The day before: the checklist

- Reread the **official workouts** and your wave time
- Pack your bag: shoes, belt, knee sleeves, **chalk**, change of clothes, warm layer, bottles, food. See [what equipment for ATHX](/blog/athx-equipment-rules)
- Plan the journey and arrival time, with a margin to find the warm-up area
- Do a short activation: a little cardio, mobility and 2 or 3 strides
- Usual, carb-rich dinner
- Go to bed at your normal time

If you're coming from far away, arrive the day before. Your event guide has the practical information: [Paris](/athx-paris-training), [Montpellier](/athx-montpellier-training) or [Marseille](/athx-marseille-training).

## Race-day morning

Wake up at least 3 hours before your wave, have a breakfast you've tested in training, arrive early. Then breathe: the work is done.

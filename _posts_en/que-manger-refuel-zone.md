Between the Strength zone and the Endurance zone, ATHX has a **Refuel zone**: a few minutes to recover, drink and get ready. Many athletes treat it as a simple break. That's a mistake: used well, it saves you precious minutes in what comes next.

To see where the Refuel zone fits in the whole race, see [the ATHX 2027 workouts](/athx-2027-workouts).

## The goal: refuel without weighing yourself down

After strength and before a long endurance effort, you need:

- **fast carbs**, to top up available fuel;
- **water and sodium**, to replace sweat losses;
- **nothing heavy**: no fat, little fibre, no large amounts of protein.

## What to bring

- a fruit pouch or a ripe banana;
- a sports drink (or water with a pinch of salt and a little sugar);
- an energy gel if you're used to them;
- a few fruit jellies.

Aim for about **30 to 40 g of carbs** and **300 to 500 ml** of fluid, in small sips.

## The golden rule: nothing new on race day

Everything you take in the Refuel zone should have been tested in training, ideally during a strength → endurance combination session. A gel your stomach can't handle can ruin your competition.

## Packing your bag

Put your food in a bag or box that's easy to open, with packets already opened if needed. The few minutes of the zone go by very quickly.

In Montpellier at the end of May or Marseille in September, plan more water and sodium than in training: detailed tips in our [ATHX Montpellier training](/athx-montpellier-training) and our [ATHX Marseille](/athx-marseille-training) guide.

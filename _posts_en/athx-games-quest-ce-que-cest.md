**ATHX Games** is a **hybrid fitness** competition that tests every physical quality in a single race of about **2h30**: maximal strength, endurance and the ability to give everything while tired. Born in the United Kingdom, it has become in just a few seasons one of the fastest-growing competitions in Europe, and it's now settling in France.

I did my first ATHX in Paris in 2026, where I finished 1st in the Strength zone. Here's everything you need to know before you get started.

## Where does ATHX come from?

ATHX was created in the United Kingdom by coaches and athletes who wanted a competition able to measure **all** physical qualities, without favouring only strength or cardio profiles. After a handful of British events in 2023, the circuit spread across Europe (Spain, France, Germany, Italy, the Netherlands…) and then to the United States, with Adidas as a partner. The 2027 season has more than 20 cities.

## The format: 6 zones in 2h30

The race runs in this order (2027 season format):

| Zone | Duration | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Warm-up zone** | 30 min | Warm-up, mobility, activation |
| **Strength zone** | 20 min | 1RM shoulder-to-overhead, 2RM back squat, 3RM deadlift |
| **Refuel zone** | 10 min | Hydration, refuelling, recovery |
| **Endurance zone** | 30 min | Run (3 km in ATHX and Pro), then as many SkiErg metres as possible, all within 24 min |
| **Recovery zone** | 30 min | Recovery before the last block |
| **Metcon X** | 30 min | Rower, dumbbells (ground-to-overhead and bench press), sandbag squats and carry, burpees, rower: 25 min cap |

### The zones that count

**Only three zones earn points**: the Strength zone, the Endurance zone and Metcon X. The Warm-up, Refuel and Recovery zones aren't scored, but they're part of the strategy: using them well means arriving fresh for the next zones.

- **Strength zone**: your score is the **total load** lifted across the three lifts. Every kilo counts. Details in [the ATHX 2027 Strength zone explained](/athx-2027-workouts/strength-zone).
- **Endurance zone**: a fixed-distance run (3 km in ATHX and Pro), then as many SkiErg metres as possible in the time left, with a 24-minute cap. Details in [the ATHX 2027 Endurance zone](/athx-2027-workouts/endurance-zone).
- **Metcon X**: the high-intensity finale, often decisive for the ranking. See [Metcon X explained](/blog/metcon-x-explained).

The details of each zone, the reps and the loads are in our guide to [the ATHX 2027 workouts](/athx-2027-workouts).

## The 3 categories

- **ATHX Lite**: the entry point. Lighter loads and adapted movements (for example step-overs instead of full jumps). Ideal for a first competition.
- **ATHX**: heavier loads and full standards, for athletes who train regularly.
- **ATHX Pro**: the heaviest loads and the most technical standards, to aim for the podium.

To choose yours: [Lite, ATHX or Pro: which category to choose](/blog/choose-athx-category), and the detailed loads in [the ATHX categories](/athx-categories).

## Solo or in pairs?

You can take part **alone** or **as a pair** (two men, two women or mixed). Pairs are a good option for a first participation: you share the experience, and motivation is easier to find during training. Format, splitting the work and training together: it's all in our guide to [preparing for ATHX as a pair](/blog/athx-doubles-training).

## ATHX or Hyrox: what's the difference?

Hyrox is a "fitness marathon": 8 × 1 km runs broken up by stations, with no breaks. ATHX is more like a **decathlon**: it separates strength, endurance and metcon, with recovery zones in between. Above all, **pure strength earns points directly**. Full comparison: [ATHX vs Hyrox](/blog/athx-vs-hyrox).

## Who is it for?

ATHX is for every regular gym-goer: gym athletes, lifters, CrossFitters and runners. It particularly suits **all-round profiles**, and it's a real opportunity for those who come from strength training: the Strength zone finally rewards strength work.

## How to prepare for it?

Good ATHX preparation trains three things in parallel:

1. **Heavy strength** on the squat, deadlift and overhead work, with loads calculated from your maxes. Start by estimating yours with the [free 1RM calculator](/1rm-calculator).
2. **Endurance**: running, SkiErg and rower, to sustain a continuous effort.
3. **Combinations**: training to produce an intense effort while already tired, like in Metcon X.

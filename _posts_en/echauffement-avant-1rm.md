A 1RM is partly won before you even touch the competition bar. Too short a warm-up, and the first attempt feels crushing. Too long or too heavy, and you arrive already tired. Here's how to do it, with a protocol built for the **30-minute ATHX Warm-up zone**.

## The principle: heavier, fewer reps

A warm-up before a max always follows the same logic: **load goes up, reps go down**. You wake up the movement, then get your nervous system used to heavy loads, without building up fatigue.

| Set | Load (% of 1RM) | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Empty bar | 8 to 10 |
| 2 | 40% | 5 |
| 3 | 55% | 3 |
| 4 | 70% | 2 |
| 5 | 80% | 1 |
| 6 | 85-87% | 1 |

Then it's time for your attempts. For a 2RM or 3RM, keep the same progression and stop the ramp-up at around 80% of the target load. Work out your steps in advance with the [1RM calculator](/1rm-calculator): you'll just have to follow your sheet.

**Example** for a target 80 kg shoulder-to-overhead: bar × 8, 32.5 kg × 5, 45 kg × 3, 55 kg × 2, 65 kg × 1, 70 kg × 1, then an opener of around 72.5 kg in competition.

## The 30-minute Warm-up zone, minute by minute

The [2027 Strength zone](/athx-2027-workouts/strength-zone) goes **shoulder-to-overhead, back squat, then deadlift**. The warm-up follows the same order.

| Minutes | Content |
| --- | --- |
| 0 to 6 | Easy rower or SkiErg, breathing should stay calm |
| 6 to 10 | Mobility: hips, ankles, shoulders, upper back |
| 10 to 12 | Activation: a few jumps, shoulder rotations with a band |
| 12 to 19 | Shoulder-to-overhead ramp-up |
| 19 to 26 | Back squat ramp-up |
| 26 to 30 | A few light to medium deadlifts, then put a layer on |

The deadlift comes last in the Strength zone: the heavy squats will already have partly prepared it. A short ramp-up is enough during the warm-up.

## Mistakes that cost you kilos

- **Too many reps** on the heavy sets: 80% × 5 in a warm-up is a real working set.
- **Testing your max in the warm-up**: save it for the competition.
- **Cooling down**: between the warm-up and the first attempt, keep a warm layer on and keep moving.
- **Improvising**: write down your steps, your opening loads and the plate changes.
- **Neglecting the end of the zone**: your last heavy set comes 5 to 10 minutes before your first attempt.

## Plan B: when the racks aren't available

It happened to me at ATHX Paris 2026: the racks in the warm-up area had no hooks. I couldn't ramp up on the squat or the press, and had to do it during the event. I tell the story in [my race report](/blog/athx-paris-2026-race-report).

If it happens to you:

1. **Dumbbells and kettlebells**: heavy goblet squats, dumbbell push presses, Romanian deadlifts.
2. **Bar from the floor**: light deadlifts, and presses starting from a light clean.
3. **Minimal ramp-up during the event**: 2 or 3 quick sets, 1 to 2 reps, big load jumps, then an opener at around 90%.

Every set you add during the event is paid for in the Endurance zone, right after.

## Practising your warm-up

The warm-up is trained like everything else: **use the same protocol before every heavy session** of your preparation. On race day, there will be nothing left to decide.

Good physical preparation can be ruined by poor eating in the 24 hours before the race. With about 2h30 of varied effort, ATHX requires you to arrive with full glycogen stores and a settled stomach.

## The day before

- **Carbs at every meal**: pasta, rice, potatoes, bread. No need to overdo the portions, but they should be the base of the plate.
- **Little fibre and fat** at dinner: avoid large amounts of raw vegetables, pulses, fried food and rich sauces.
- **Regular hydration** throughout the day, with a little salt in your meals.
- **No alcohol**, and dinner not too late so you sleep well.

Example dinner: white rice, chicken or fish, cooked courgettes, stewed fruit.

## The morning

- Eat **2h30 to 3h before** your wave starts.
- Choose an easy-to-digest, carb-rich meal: white bread or oats, honey or jam, a banana, a little yoghurt or an egg.
- Drink 400 to 600 ml of water during and after breakfast, then small sips until the start.
- Your usual coffee is fine, if you're used to it.

## Just before the start

A banana or a fruit pouch 30 to 45 minutes before the Warm-up zone tops up the tank without weighing on your stomach.

Travelling a long way for [ATHX Paris](/athx-paris-training)? Arrive the day before and keep your usual eating habits: dinner at an unfamiliar restaurant the night before the race is an unnecessary risk.

## The golden rule

**Nothing new on race day.** Test your competition breakfast before one of your big training sessions.

Many athletes who sign up for ATHX come from CrossFit. It makes sense: you'll find strength, machines and linked efforts. Yet the two formats don't reward **exactly the same thing**. Here's what changes, and how to adapt your training.

## Two opposite approaches: the unknown vs the known

CrossFit is built on **constantly varied workouts**. In competition, the events change every year and are often revealed shortly before: that's the whole principle of the Open, whose workouts are announced week by week. It tests the ability to be good **everywhere**, including at the unexpected.

ATHX does the opposite. The [ATHX 2027 workouts](/athx-2027-workouts) are **published for the whole season** and identical at every event, in Paris, Montpellier or Marseille. Your score is directly comparable with any other athlete's, and you can **prepare precisely** for each event.

| | CrossFit (competition) | ATHX |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Workouts | Varied, often announced shortly before | Known for the whole season |
| Format | Several independent events | 6 zones over about 2h30, 3 of them scored |
| Movements | Olympic lifting, gymnastics, machines, loads | Fundamental movements and machines |
| Maximal strength | Sometimes tested, depending on the year | Always tested (Strength zone) |
| Breaks | Vary by event | Built-in recovery zones (Refuel, Recovery) |
| Categories | Rx, Scaled, age groups | Lite, ATHX, Pro, solo or in pairs |

## Technique: less gymnastics, more fundamentals

This is the most visible difference. CrossFit requires very technical movements: **snatch, clean and jerk, muscle-ups, handstand push-ups**… An athlete limited on these movements can lose an entire event.

ATHX removes most of that complexity. In 2027, you'll find:

- in the **Strength zone**: [shoulder-to-overhead](/athx-movements/shoulder-to-overhead), [back squat](/athx-movements/back-squat) and [deadlift](/athx-movements/deadlift);
- in the **Endurance zone**: a run then the [SkiErg](/athx-movements/skierg);
- in **Metcon X**: [rower](/athx-movements/rower), dumbbells, sandbag squats and carry, burpees.

These are movements every gym-goer knows. So the ranking is decided more by **strength and engine** than by technical mastery. All the standards are detailed in our guide to [the ATHX movements](/athx-movements).

## Maximal strength: a real test at ATHX

In CrossFit, maximal strength is only tested in some years, often in a short format. At ATHX, it has **its own 20-minute zone**: 1RM shoulder-to-overhead, 2RM back squat and 3RM deadlift. The score is the **total load**, every kilo counts.

For a CrossFitter used to heavy loads, it's often the zone where they score the most points. The key is choosing your attempts well: estimate your loads with the [1RM calculator](/1rm-calculator) and read our guide to [the Strength zone](/athx-2027-workouts/strength-zone).

## Endurance: the CrossFitter's frequent weak spot

CrossFit WODs rarely last more than 20 minutes, and the running is often short. ATHX requires you to **last about 2h30**, with a 24-minute Endurance zone: a 3 km run then as many SkiErg metres as possible.

That's where many CrossFitters lose places. Two adjustments are often enough:

1. **Two runs a week**, including a long one at an easy pace;
2. **Regular SkiErg work**, in intervals and 10-minute blocks.

To see how many metres you can expect, try the [Endurance zone calculator](/athx-endurance-zone-calculator), and read [how to build an engine that lasts 2h30](/blog/running-rowing-athx-endurance).

## Metcon X: familiar ground, but on tired legs

[Metcon X](/athx-2027-workouts/metcon-x) looks like a CrossFit WOD: rower, dumbbells, sandbag, burpees, with a 25-minute cap. The difference is that it comes **after two hours of effort**. It rewards those who managed the previous zones, not just the most explosive. See [Metcon X explained](/blog/metcon-x-explained).

## So, ATHX or CrossFit?

It's not either/or: ATHX Games has in fact become an **official CrossFit partner event series**. CrossFit is an excellent training base for ATHX. If you like workouts known in advance, heavy loads and a ranking you can compare from one event to the next, ATHX is for you.

Doing ATHX as a pair is the ideal formula for a first competition: you share the training, the pressure and the pride of crossing the line. But pairs have their own rules and their own strategy. Here's how to prepare to get the best out of it.

## Pairs in brief

You can register as a pair in all three categories (**Lite, ATHX and Pro**), men, women or mixed. The format stays the 6 zones and 2h30 race, but each scored zone has its own rules for pairs. Pairs also complete **a higher total volume** than individuals, especially in Metcon X.

| Zone | In pairs (2027 season) |
| --- | --- |
| Strength zone | Each athlete makes their own attempts on all three lifts |
| Endurance zone | Both start the run together, the first to finish can start the SkiErg |
| Metcon X | Reps shared freely, sandbag carry split equally (30 m each) |

> The official workouts are the reference: reread the standards and pairs rules for your event before the competition.

## Strength zone: two bars, one score

In the Strength zone, **each athlete lifts their own loads**: 1RM shoulder-to-overhead, 2RM back squat and 3RM deadlift. The pair's score is based on both athletes' loads: the weaker athlete's strength counts as much as the stronger one's.

What changes as a pair:

- **Plan your attempts together**, the day before, with the [1RM calculator](/1rm-calculator). Each of you knows the other's loads and can push them if they hesitate.
- **Coach each other**: between attempts, whoever isn't lifting checks the load, keeps time and gives the technical cues.
- **Organise the warm-up**: share the equipment and take turns. My [Paris race report](/blog/athx-paris-2026-race-report) shows how costly a botched warm-up can be.

All the details of the three lifts are in [the ATHX 2027 Strength zone explained](/athx-2027-workouts/strength-zone).

## Endurance zone: start together, finish on the SkiErg

Both athletes **start the run together**. As soon as the first one has covered the distance, they can start the SkiErg. Time saved by the better runner becomes extra ski time for the pair.

- **Don't wait for each other** during the run: each runs at their own pace.
- **The better runner opens the SkiErg**, so they must be able to go hard as soon as they arrive.
- **Work out your paces in advance** with the [Endurance zone calculator](/athx-endurance-zone-calculator), to know how many metres to aim for.

The full format is detailed in [the ATHX 2027 Endurance zone](/athx-2027-workouts/endurance-zone).

## Metcon X: it all comes down to the split

This is the zone where pairs really make the difference. Rower, dumbbell, sandbag and burpee reps **are shared however you like**, with one exception: **the sandbag carry is split equally**, 30 m each. While one athlete works, the other recovers.

### Building your split plan

1. **List the movements** of the [2027 Metcon X](/athx-2027-workouts/metcon-x) and note who is stronger on each one.
2. **Give more reps to the more efficient athlete**, without completely draining the other: a handover that collapses costs more than a balanced split.
3. **Prefer short sets and quick handovers** (for example 5 to 10 reps each) to keep the intensity high.
4. **Agree on a signal** for changes: a tap on the shoulder, a word. No discussion during the effort.
5. **Write the plan down** and keep it in mind. You improvise badly after two hours of effort.

The sandbag often catches people out: work on it together with [sandbag technique](/blog/sandbag-technique).

## Choosing your partner well

- **Same category**: the Metcon X loads are the same for both of you. Aim for a category the weaker athlete can handle. See [which category to choose](/blog/choose-athx-category).
- **Complementary profiles**: a strength athlete and an endurance athlete complement each other well in Metcon X.
- **Shared commitment**: same number of sessions a week and same training start date. A pair where one person trains half as much rarely ends well.

## Training together

- **Strength, each at your own loads**: same session, but each of you works with percentages of your own PRs.
- **Endurance together** when possible: a shared long run is easier to stick to.
- **A pairs Metcon simulation** every one to two weeks, with your split plan and your signals. That's where the seconds are won.
- **Combinations** of strength then running, like on race day: that's the pre-fatigue training described in [managing the strength → endurance transition](/blog/strength-to-endurance-transition).

The [ATHX PREP program](/#cta) works for pairs: each of you gets loads calculated from your own PRs, and the endurance and Metcon sessions are done together (program written in French). To start together this week, [try the free ATHX program](/free-athx-training-week).

## Common mistakes in pairs

- **Choosing the stronger athlete's category** and suffering through the whole Metcon X.
- **Improvising the split** on race day.
- **Waiting for each other during the run**, when the first to finish should start the SkiErg.
- **Training separately right to the end** without ever testing the handovers.

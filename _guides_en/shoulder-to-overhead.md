## The standard

The bar starts **resting on the shoulders** (rack position) and must finish **overhead, arms locked out**, with the body aligned (hips and knees extended). Any technique is allowed. It's the first lift of the [2027 Strength zone](/athx-2027-workouts/strength-zone), tested as a 1RM.

## Three ways to do it

| Technique | How it works | Possible load |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Strict press | Arms only, legs still | Baseline |
| Push press | Small knee dip, then an explosive drive | Often +15 to 30% |
| Jerk | Leg drive, then dropping under the bar | Heaviest, but most technical |

## The push press step by step

1. **Position**: bar on the shoulders, elbows slightly in front of the bar, feet hip-width apart.
2. **Dip**: drop a few centimetres by bending the knees, torso upright, heels down.
3. **Drive**: extend the legs explosively, the drive launches the bar.
4. **Finish**: press with the arms to lockout, your head goes "through the window" under the bar.

## Common mistakes

- **Dip too deep or forward**: the drive goes in the wrong direction and the bar drifts away.
- **Arms not locked out** at the end of the movement: the rep is rejected.
- **Arched back** to compensate: a sign the load is too heavy or your core isn't braced enough.

## Exercises to progress

- Strict press: the strength foundation.
- Push press in sets of 2 to 3 reps between 80 and 90% of 1RM.
- Core work and shoulder and thoracic mobility.

## ATHX Lite in brief

The **Lite** category keeps everything that makes ATHX (the 6 zones, a 2h30 race, the atmosphere), with an adapted physical demand. It's the best way to discover the competition.

| Zone | In ATHX Lite |
| --- | --- |
| Strength zone | 1RM shoulder-to-overhead, 2RM squat, 3RM deadlift, free loads |
| Endurance zone | Shorter run, then SkiErg (24 min cap) |
| Metcon X | 40 / 30 rower cal, 30 reps per station, 15 / 7.5 kg dumbbell, 30 / 20 kg sandbag |

## Metcon X in Lite

Loads men / women:

- 40 / 30 rower calories
- 30 dumbbell ground-to-overhead at 15 / 7.5 kg
- 30 dumbbell bench presses at 15 / 7.5 kg
- 30 sandbag squats at 30 / 20 kg
- 60 m sandbag carry at 30 / 20 kg
- 30 burpees over the bench
- 40 / 30 rower calories

## The required level

- Perform the competition movements cleanly, especially the full squat and the deadlift: see [the ATHX movements](/athx-movements).
- Run for about twenty minutes without stopping.
- Get through a 20 to 25-minute circuit without blowing up.

No need for impressive loads: in the Strength zone, **everyone chooses their own attempts**.

## Preparing for ATHX Lite

Allow **12 weeks** with 3 to 4 sessions a week: 2 strength sessions, 1 to 2 endurance sessions and, in the second half, a Metcon X simulation. Start by estimating your loads with the [1RM calculator](/1rm-calculator), then see a concrete example with the [free ATHX training week](/free-athx-training-week).

Torn between Lite and the next category up? Compare with [ATHX](/athx-categories/athx) or read [which category to choose](/blog/choose-athx-category).

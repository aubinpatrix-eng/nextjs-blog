## The SkiErg at ATHX 2027

In the [2027 Endurance zone](/athx-2027-workouts/endurance-zone), you run a fixed distance then rack up **as many SkiErg metres as possible** until the 24-minute cap. Every metre counts towards the score.

How many metres can you expect? Enter your running pace and your pace per 500 m in the [SkiErg metres calculator](/athx-endurance-zone-calculator).

## The technique step by step

1. **Starting position**: standing, arms extended overhead, handles slightly in front of your head.
2. **Pull**: arms stay almost straight, you hinge at the hips and bend the knees while engaging your core, as if "crunching" downwards.
3. **Finish**: hands pass alongside the thighs, the arms finish the push backwards.
4. **Recovery**: stand back up as the arms rise, without rushing: this is your recovery moment.

## Common mistakes

- **Pulling with the arms only**: exhausting and inefficient.
- **Bending the knees like a squat**: the movement comes from the hips, the knees follow.
- **Stroke rate too high**: long, powerful strokes are better.

## Sample sessions

- **Technique**: 5 × 2 min at an easy pace, focusing on the hips.
- **Power**: 8 × 250 m fast, 1 min rest.
- **ATHX-specific**: 3 km run then 12 to 15 min of SkiErg at race pace.

## The standard and the format

The deadlift is the last lift of the [2027 Strength zone](/athx-2027-workouts/strength-zone), tested as a **3RM**. Athletes get **two one-minute windows**, with one minute of rest in between. In each window, three reps **touch-and-go or as three singles**. The score is **the average of the two loads**.

At the top of the rep: hips and knees locked out, shoulders behind the bar.

## The technique step by step

1. **Set-up**: feet hip-width apart, bar over mid-foot.
2. **Grip**: hands just outside the legs, arms straight.
3. **Build tension**: neutral back, chest up, "take the slack out" of the bar before it leaves the floor.
4. **Pull**: push the floor away with the legs, bar dragging up the shins then the thighs.
5. **Lockout**: hips forward, without leaning the torso back.

## The two-window strategy

Since the average counts, **a miss in the second window is very costly**. Pick two similar, safe loads, for example your estimated 3RM minus 5 kg, then your 3RM. Singles with a few seconds of rest are often more reliable than three reps in a row.

## Common mistakes

- **Rounded back**: lighten the load, work on bracing.
- **Bar drifting away from the legs**: the load becomes much heavier.
- **Hips shooting up too fast**: the back does all the work.

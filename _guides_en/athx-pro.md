## ATHX Pro in brief

| Zone | In ATHX Pro |
| --- | --- |
| Strength zone | 1RM shoulder-to-overhead, 2RM squat, 3RM deadlift, free loads |
| Endurance zone | 3 km run, then max SkiErg metres (24 min cap) |
| Metcon X | 60 / 45 rower cal, 45 to 60 reps, 2 dumbbells of 22.5 / 15 kg, 70 / 50 kg sandbag |

## Metcon X in ATHX Pro

Loads men / women:

- 60 / 45 rower calories
- 45 ground-to-overhead reps with **two dumbbells**, 22.5 / 15 kg each
- 60 dumbbell bench presses at 22.5 / 15 kg
- 45 sandbag squats at 70 / 50 kg
- 60 m sandbag carry at 70 / 50 kg
- 45 burpees over the bench
- 60 / 45 rower calories

With two dumbbells, the *ground-to-overhead* becomes a real strength-endurance test: see [the dumbbell technique](/athx-movements/dumbbell-ground-to-overhead).

## The required level

- **High strength**: a 70 kg sandbag (men) in the squat should stay a working load, not a max.
- **A complete engine**: a fast 3 km then a powerful SkiErg, see [the Endurance zone](/athx-2027-workouts/endurance-zone).
- **Flawless standards**: in Pro, the judges are uncompromising on range of motion. Review [the ATHX movements](/athx-movements).

## Preparing for ATHX Pro

Pro preparation requires **12 structured weeks** with 5 to 6 sessions a week, precisely calculated loads and full simulations. That's where a program based on your PRs makes the difference: [estimate your maxes](/1rm-calculator) then discover the ATHX PREP program.

## The ATHX category in brief

It's the **standard** category, the one with the most athletes.

| Zone | In the ATHX category |
| --- | --- |
| Strength zone | 1RM shoulder-to-overhead, 2RM squat, 3RM deadlift, free loads |
| Endurance zone | 3 km run, then max SkiErg metres (24 min cap) |
| Metcon X | 60 / 45 rower cal, 45 to 60 reps per station, 20 / 12.5 kg dumbbell, 50 / 30 kg sandbag |

## Metcon X in the ATHX category

Loads men / women:

- 60 / 45 rower calories
- 45 dumbbell ground-to-overhead at 20 / 12.5 kg
- 60 dumbbell bench presses at 20 / 12.5 kg
- 45 sandbag squats at 50 / 30 kg
- 60 m sandbag carry at 50 / 30 kg
- 45 burpees over the bench
- 60 / 45 rower calories

The upper-body volume (45 + 60 dumbbell reps) is the real challenge: see [the 2027 Metcon X](/athx-2027-workouts/metcon-x).

## The required level

- Hold **3 km at a solid pace** then ski hard: see [the Endurance zone](/athx-2027-workouts/endurance-zone).
- Get through the Metcon X stations by breaking up sets smartly, without going to failure.
- Solid strength foundations, to score points in the Strength zone.

## Preparing for the ATHX category

Allow **12 weeks** with 4 to 5 sessions a week: heavy strength, endurance (running and SkiErg) and Metcon X simulations. The ATHX PREP program calculates your loads from your PRs: [estimate them with the 1RM calculator](/1rm-calculator).

Aiming higher? Compare with [ATHX Pro](/athx-categories/athx-pro). Prefer a more accessible first experience? See [ATHX Lite](/athx-categories/athx-lite).

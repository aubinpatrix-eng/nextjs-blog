## The movement in the 2027 Metcon X

Third station of the [2027 Metcon X](/athx-2027-workouts/metcon-x):

| Category | Reps | Dumbbells (M / W) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lite | 30 | 15 / 7.5 kg |
| ATHX | 60 | 20 / 12.5 kg |
| Pro | 60 | 22.5 / 15 kg |

A rep counts with the **dumbbells down by the chest, then arms locked out at the top**. Check the details in the official standards.

## The technique step by step

1. **Set-up**: sit at the end of the bench, dumbbells on your thighs, then lie back bringing them to your chest.
2. **Position**: feet on the floor, shoulder blades squeezed, slight natural arch.
3. **Lowering**: elbows at about 45° from the body, dumbbells down to the sides of the chest.
4. **Press**: push up until the arms lock out.
5. **Rest**: between sets, put the dumbbells on the floor or your thighs, without fully getting up.

## The strategy: never go to failure

Going to failure on a set can cost you a minute of forced rest. Break it up from the start, for example **5 sets of 12 with 10 to 15 seconds of rest** in ATHX. The time saved with short rests is worth far more than one big set.

## Common mistakes

- **Incomplete range of motion** at the bottom: rep rejected.
- **Elbows flared too wide**: the shoulders suffer and tire faster.
- **Starting with big sets** then grinding out sets of 3.

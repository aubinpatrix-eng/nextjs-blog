## The competition format

- **ATHX Games**: a hybrid fitness competition of about 2h30, split into 6 zones. Born in the United Kingdom, it has three events in France in 2027: [Paris](/athx-paris-training), [Montpellier](/athx-montpellier-training) and [Marseille](/athx-marseille-training). See [What is ATHX Games?](/blog/what-is-athx-games)
- **Zone**: each of the 6 parts of the competition. Three are scored (Strength, Endurance, Metcon X), three are for preparing and recovering.
- **Warm-up zone**: 30 minutes of warm-up before the Strength zone. Not scored.
- **Strength zone**: 20 minutes to do a 1RM shoulder-to-overhead, a 2RM back squat and a 3RM deadlift. The score is the total load. See [the 2027 Strength zone](/athx-2027-workouts/strength-zone).
- **Refuel zone**: 10 minutes to drink, eat and recover. See [what to eat in the Refuel zone](/blog/what-to-eat-refuel-zone).
- **Endurance zone**: a fixed-distance run (3 km in ATHX and Pro), then as many SkiErg metres as possible, within a 24-minute cap. See [the 2027 Endurance zone](/athx-2027-workouts/endurance-zone).
- **Recovery zone**: 30 minutes of recovery before Metcon X. Not scored.
- **Metcon X**: the last scored block, a circuit on the rower, dumbbells, sandbag and burpees, with a 25-minute cap. See [the 2027 Metcon X](/athx-2027-workouts/metcon-x).
- **Time cap**: the time limit of an event. If you don't finish before it, your score is what you'd done at that point.
- **Wave**: the start time slot given to each athlete or pair.
- **Scoring**: how the scored zones are assessed: total load in the Strength zone, distance in the Endurance zone, time in Metcon X. Each athlete gets a place in each zone, and the overall ranking adds up those three places: the lowest total wins. See [ATHX scores by level](/athx-results).

## The categories

- **ATHX Lite**: the entry category, with reduced loads and distances. See [ATHX Lite](/athx-categories/athx-lite).
- **ATHX**: the intermediate category, for regular athletes. See [the ATHX category](/athx-categories/athx).
- **ATHX Pro**: the most demanding category, with the heaviest loads. See [ATHX Pro](/athx-categories/athx-pro).
- **Solo / pairs**: each category can be done alone or as a pair, men, women or mixed. In pairs, the effort is shared according to the rules of each zone.

To choose, read [Lite, ATHX or Pro: which category to choose](/blog/choose-athx-category).

## Strength

- **RM (repetition maximum)**: the **1RM** is the heaviest load you can lift once, the **2RM** twice in a row, the **3RM** three times. Calculate yours with the [1RM calculator](/1rm-calculator).
- **PR** (*personal record*): your best performance to date on a lift.
- **% of 1RM**: a training load expressed as a percentage of your max. 80% of a 100 kg squat is 80 kg.
- **Attempt**: each try at a given load in the Strength zone. A missed attempt doesn't count, hence the importance of choosing your first bar well.
- **Shoulder-to-overhead**: taking the bar from the shoulders to overhead, arms straight. In 2027, leg drive is allowed: push press or jerk. See [the shoulder-to-overhead](/athx-movements/shoulder-to-overhead).
- **Push press**: a press with leg drive, without dropping under the bar.
- **Jerk**: you launch the bar with the legs then drop under it, arms straight. More technical, it lets you lift heavier.
- **Back squat**: a squat with the bar on the upper back. See [the back squat](/athx-movements/back-squat).
- **Deadlift**: lifting the bar from the floor to standing, hips and knees locked out. See [the deadlift](/athx-movements/deadlift).
- **Standard**: the criteria a movement must meet to count (range of motion, lockout…). A movement that doesn't meet them is a **no rep**.

## Endurance and metcon

- **Hybrid fitness**: competitions mixing strength, running and functional exercises, like ATHX, Hyrox or DEKA. See [ATHX vs Hyrox](/blog/athx-vs-hyrox), [ATHX vs DEKA](/blog/athx-vs-deka) and [ATHX vs CrossFit](/blog/athx-vs-crossfit).
- **Metcon** (*metabolic conditioning*): an intense circuit that works your cardio and muscles at the same time. See [Metcon X explained](/blog/metcon-x-explained).
- **SkiErg**: a machine that reproduces the pole push of cross-country skiing. It finishes the Endurance zone in 2027. See [SkiErg technique](/athx-movements/skierg).
- **Rower** (*row erg*): a rowing machine, used in Metcon X. See [rower technique](/athx-movements/rower).
- **Pace per 500 m**: the time taken to cover 500 m on the SkiErg or rower, for example 2:10/500 m. It's the reference for managing your effort. Calculate your metres with the [Endurance zone calculator](/athx-endurance-zone-calculator).
- **Calories (cal)**: some events are counted in the calories shown by the machine, not in metres.
- **Ground-to-overhead**: taking a load from the floor to overhead, arms straight. In Metcon X, it's done with a dumbbell. See [the dumbbell ground-to-overhead](/athx-movements/dumbbell-ground-to-overhead).
- **Sandbag carry**: carrying a weighted bag over a set distance. See [sandbag technique](/blog/sandbag-technique).
- **Burpee**: going from standing to lying on the floor, then getting up with a jump. A classic at the end of metcons.

## Training

- **RPE** (*rating of perceived exertion*): how hard the effort feels, rated from 1 to 10. An RPE of 8 means you have about two reps left in reserve.
- **Deload**: a lighter week to recover and arrive fresh, often right before the competition. See [the week before ATHX](/blog/athx-race-week).
- **Finisher**: a short, intense block at the end of a session, for example on the SkiErg or rower.
- **EMOM** (*every minute on the minute*): an exercise done at the start of every minute, resting for the rest of the minute.
- **AMRAP** (*as many rounds as possible*): the maximum number of rounds or reps in a given time.
- **Threshold**: the fastest pace you can hold for about an hour. Threshold sessions improve running endurance.
- **Simulation**: a session that reproduces one or more competition zones, to get used to the sequence.

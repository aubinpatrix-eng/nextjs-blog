The 2027 ATHX season changes the **Strength zone**. The 2026 formats (1RM strict press, 3RM squat, 5RM deadlift) give way to **1RM, 2RM and 3RM**: fewer reps, heavier loads. Pure strength matters even more. I won this zone in Paris in 2026: here's how I'd approach it this season.

## The three lifts

1. **1RM shoulder-to-overhead**: take the bar from the shoulders to overhead, arms locked out. Unlike the 2026 strict press, **leg drive is allowed** (push press, jerk). Technique in detail in [the shoulder-to-overhead](/athx-movements/shoulder-to-overhead).
2. **2RM back squat**: two full reps, hip crease below the top of the knee. See [the back squat](/athx-movements/back-squat).
3. **3RM deadlift**: three reps, touch-and-go or as three singles, within a one-minute window. See [the deadlift](/athx-movements/deadlift).

## How it runs and how it's scored

The 20 minutes are split into **timed windows**, one per lift, with time to build up. For the deadlift, athletes get **two one-minute windows** (with one minute of rest in between): the score is **the average of the two loads**.

**The zone score is the total load** across the three lifts. Every kilo counts, on each of the three exercises.

What total should you aim for? In 2026, in France, it took about 280 kg for women and 430 kg for men to reach the top 10% of the Strength zone (2026 format). All the levels are on our [ATHX results](/athx-results) page.

> The exact length of each window and the number of attempts are set out in the official workouts for the season.

## Choosing your attempts

The classic mistake: opening too heavy and missing. A missed attempt scores nothing. It all starts with a good warm-up: see [warming up before a 1RM](/blog/warm-up-before-1rm), and [the allowed equipment](/blog/athx-equipment-rules) (belt, knee sleeves, chalk). Then, a simple strategy:

- **1st attempt**: about 90% of your estimated max, a load you're sure to make, even under stress;
- **2nd attempt**: your current max;
- **3rd attempt** (if there's time): +2.5 to 5 kg if the previous one went up easily.

For the deadlift, aim for **two similar loads**: the average counts, so a miss in the second window is costly. Estimate your 1RM, 2RM and 3RM with the [1RM calculator](/1rm-calculator).

## Training for this format

- **Train heavy with low reps**: sets of 1 to 3 reps between 85 and 92% of your 1RM in the final weeks.
- **Learn the push press and the jerk**: if you only did strict presses, you're leaving kilos on the table.
- **Simulate the windows**: time your build-ups to know how many sets you can fit into the time allowed.
- **Save some energy**: the Strength zone is followed by endurance. Read [managing the strength → endurance transition](/blog/strength-to-endurance-transition).

## Three categories, one format

Every category goes through **the same 6 zones, with the same durations** (see [the ATHX 2027 workouts](/athx-2027-workouts)). What changes is the physical demand.

| | ATHX Lite | ATHX | ATHX Pro |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Who for? | First competition, intermediate level | Regular athletes | Experienced competitors |
| Strength zone | Free loads | Free loads | Free loads |
| Run (Endurance) | Shorter distance | 3 km | 3 km |
| Metcon X | Reduced volume and loads | Standard | Heaviest loads |
| Metcon X dumbbell (M / W) | 15 / 7.5 kg | 20 / 12.5 kg | 22.5 / 15 kg (2 dumbbells overhead) |
| Sandbag (M / W) | 30 / 20 kg | 50 / 30 kg | 70 / 50 kg |

## Metcon X loads by category

Metcon X is where the gap between categories is clearest: from **30 to 60 reps** per station and loads that can double. The full station-by-station detail is in [the 2027 Metcon X](/athx-2027-workouts/metcon-x).

## And in the Strength zone?

No set load: **everyone chooses their own attempts** and the score is the total lifted. You are then ranked within your category and division. See [the ATHX 2027 Strength zone](/athx-2027-workouts/strength-zone), and the [average ATHX scores](/athx-results) to know which total to aim for.

## Which category should you choose?

- **You're new to competition** or one of your qualities (strength or cardio) is lagging → [ATHX Lite](/athx-categories/athx-lite).
- **You train regularly** and are comfortable with both strength and endurance → [ATHX](/athx-categories/athx).
- **You're aiming for the leaderboard** and the Pro loads are a moderate fraction of your maxes → [ATHX Pro](/athx-categories/athx-pro).

The detailed criteria are in [Lite, ATHX or Pro: which category to choose](/blog/choose-athx-category). The ATHX PREP program adjusts intensities to your target category, on top of starting from your PRs.

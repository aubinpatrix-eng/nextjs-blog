## The movement in the 2027 Metcon X

It's the second station of the [2027 Metcon X](/athx-2027-workouts/metcon-x), right after the rower:

| Category | Reps | Load (M / W) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lite | 30 | 1 dumbbell of 15 / 7.5 kg |
| ATHX | 45 | 1 dumbbell of 20 / 12.5 kg |
| Pro | 45 | 2 dumbbells of 22.5 / 15 kg |

A rep counts when the dumbbell, starting from the floor, is **stable overhead with the arm locked out**, hips and knees extended.

## Two possible techniques

### The snatch

The dumbbell goes from the floor to overhead in a single movement, thanks to an explosive hip extension. It's the fastest with moderate loads.

### Via the shoulder (clean and jerk)

First bring the dumbbell to the shoulder, then drive it overhead with the legs. Slower, but safer with heavy loads, especially in Pro.

## Key points

- **The legs and hips do the work**, not the shoulder: you'll need it for the 60 bench presses that follow.
- **Neutral back** when picking up the dumbbell.
- **Break it up**: for example sets of 8 to 10, with a few seconds of rest.

## Training

Include ground-to-overhead sets in circuits with the rower and bench press, to reproduce the competition sequence. See also [the dumbbell bench press](/athx-movements/dumbbell-bench-press) and [the shoulder-to-overhead](/athx-movements/shoulder-to-overhead) for overhead strength.

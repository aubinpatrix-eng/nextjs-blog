## Before you start

This week is **taken from the ATHX PREP program** (week 1): 4 full-body strength sessions, each finished with a **SkiErg or rower finisher**, and one **ATHX Metcon** session. It gives you a concrete look at the structure, intensity and coaching cues of the full program (the full program is written in French; this free week is in English).

| Day | Session |
| --- | --- |
| Monday | Full body 1 + SkiErg |
| Tuesday | Full body 2 + rower |
| Wednesday | Rest |
| Thursday | Full body 3 + SkiErg / rower EMOM |
| Friday | Full body 4 + rower / SkiErg |
| Saturday | ATHX Metcon |
| Sunday | Rest |

### Reading the intensity

- **RPE**: perceived difficulty out of 10. RPE 8 = you had about 2 reps left in reserve; RPE 10 = failure.
- **% of 1RM**: a percentage of your maximum load. Estimate your maxes with the [1RM calculator](/1rm-calculator), then round to 2.5 kg.
- **Warm-up**: the number of ramp-up sets before the working sets.
- **Top set**: a single heavy set, followed by lighter sets (*back-off*).

## Session 1 (Monday): squat and overhead press

| Exercise | Warm-up | Sets × reps | Intensity | Rest | Cue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Back squat (top set) | 4 | 1 × 5 | RPE 7.5 | 3-4 min | Controlled tempo, explosive drive |
| Back squat | — | 2 × 8 | ≈ 70%* | 3-4 min | Same back angle on every rep |
| Overhead press | 2 | 3 × 8 | ≈ 70%* | 2-3 min | Start from a dead stop on each rep |
| Glute ham raise | 1 | 3 × 8-10 | RPE 7 | 1-2 min | Keep the hips aligned |
| Helms row | 1 | 3 × 12-15 | RPE 9 | 1-2 min | Strict form, elbows back at 45° |
| Hammer curl | — | 3 × 20-25 | RPE 10 | 1-2 min | Elbows fixed, squeeze the handle hard |

**SkiErg finisher**: 5 × 250 m, 1 min rest. *Solid, steady pace: all 5 blocks in the same time.*

## Session 2 (Tuesday): deadlift and bench press

| Exercise | Warm-up | Sets × reps | Intensity | Rest | Cue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Deadlift | 4 | 3 × 6 | ≈ 75%* | 3-5 min | Conventional or sumo |
| Bench press (top set) | 4 | 1 × 3 | RPE 8.5 | 4-5 min | Hard set: keep 1 to 2 reps in reserve |
| Pause bench press | — | 2 × 10 | ≈ 67%* | 2-3 min | 1 s pause on the chest |
| Hip abduction | — | 3 × 15-20 | RPE 9 | 1-2 min | Machine or band, 1 s hold at the top |
| Weighted pull-ups | 1 | 3 × 5-8 | RPE 8 | 3-4 min | Grip 1.5 × shoulder width, chest to the bar |
| Standing calf raises | 1 | 3 × 8-10 | RPE 9 | 2-3 min | 1/2 s pause at the bottom of each rep |

**Rower finisher**: 4 × 300 m, 1 min rest. *Leg drive first, 26-30 strokes/min.*

## Session 3 (Thursday): squat and dips

| Exercise | Warm-up | Sets × reps | Intensity | Rest | Cue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Back squat | 4 | 3 × 4 | ≈ 80%* | 3-4 min | Keep your upper back pressed into the bar |
| Weighted dips | 2 | 3 × 8 | RPE 8 | 2-3 min | Full range of motion, shoulders down |
| Hanging leg raises | — | 3 × 10-12 | RPE 9 | 1-2 min | Knees to chest, controlled; straight legs to make it harder |
| Pull-over | 1 | 3 × 12-15 | RPE 8 | 1-2 min | Dumbbell, rope or band: stretch and squeeze the lats |
| Incline dumbbell curl | 1 | 3 × 12-15 | RPE 9 | 1-2 min | One arm at a time, start with the weaker arm |
| Face pull | — | 4 × 15-20 | RPE 9 | 1-2 min | Squeeze the shoulder blades as you pull |

**SkiErg + rower finisher**: 10-min EMOM: odd minutes 12 / 10 cal SkiErg, even minutes 12 / 10 cal rower (M / W). *If you can't keep up with the minute, take off 2 calories.*

## Session 4 (Friday): pause deadlift and pause bench press

| Exercise | Warm-up | Sets × reps | Intensity | Rest | Cue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pause deadlift | 4 | 4 × 2 | ≈ 75%* | 3-4 min | 2 s pause just below the knees |
| Pause bench press | 3 | 3 × 5 | ≈ 75%* | 2-3 min | Clear pause on the chest, then explode |
| Chest-supported T-bar row or Pendlay row | 1 | 3 × 10 | RPE 7 | 2-3 min | Pull with your back, not your arms |
| Nordic ham curl (or leg curl) | — | 3 × 6-8 | RPE 8 | 1-2 min | Lower as slowly as possible |
| Dumbbell shrug | — | 3 × 20-25 | RPE 9 | 1-2 min | Stretch at the bottom, squeeze hard at the top |

**Rower + SkiErg finisher**: 3 rounds: 250 m rower + 250 m SkiErg, 2 min rest. *Race pace: the transition must be fast.*

## Session 5 (Saturday): ATHX Metcon

A session to prepare for the [Endurance zone](/athx-2027-workouts/endurance-zone) and [Metcon X](/athx-2027-workouts/metcon-x) sequence. Use the loads for your category (see [the ATHX categories](/athx-categories)), or slightly lighter if your technique breaks down.

- **Warm-up**: 10 min easy rowing + hip and shoulder mobility
- **Block 1: run + SkiErg**: 2 × (1 km run + 500 m SkiErg), 3 min rest. Solid but controlled pace, like in the Endurance zone.
- **Block 2: mini Metcon X**: 3 rounds at moderate intensity, 3 min rest between rounds: 15 / 12 cal rower, 10 dumbbell ground-to-overhead, 12 dumbbell bench presses, 10 sandbag squats, 30 m sandbag carry, 8 burpees over a bench.
- **Cool-down**: 5 min walking or very easy rowing, stretching

Technique: [SkiErg](/athx-movements/skierg), [rower](/athx-movements/rower), [ground-to-overhead](/athx-movements/dumbbell-ground-to-overhead) and [dumbbell bench press](/athx-movements/dumbbell-bench-press).

## And after week 1?

Over 12 weeks, the program alternates full-body weeks and upper / lower weeks, progresses the loads and RPEs, varies the exercises (pauses, block pulls, variations) and includes a deload before the competition. That's exactly what the ATHX PREP program contains, with **every load calculated from your PRs**.

Got a competition in sight? Line up your week 1 with the right date using [ATHX Paris training](/athx-paris-training), [ATHX Montpellier](/athx-montpellier-training) or the [ATHX Marseille program](/athx-marseille-training).

*\* Indicative percentages: adjust them to how you feel and to the target RPE.*

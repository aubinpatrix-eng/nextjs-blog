## Why technique matters so much

### Rejected reps are costly

In competition, a judge validates every rep. Incomplete range of motion (squat too high, arms not locked out) and the rep has to be redone. Over 60 bench presses, a few rejected reps can cost you places.

### Good technique saves energy

On the rower and the SkiErg, poor technique tires the arms instead of using the legs and hips. Over a 2h30 race, the difference is huge.

## How to work on technique

- **Film yourself** from the side on the strength lifts and compare with the standards.
- **Go light before loading up**: technique first, load second.
- **Practise while tired**: it's at the end of Metcon X that technique breaks down. Include the movements in circuits.

To know which loads to aim for, start with the [1RM calculator](/1rm-calculator), then see [the ATHX 2027 workouts](/athx-2027-workouts) to find where each movement comes in.

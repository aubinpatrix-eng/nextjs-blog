## The 2027 workout

Metcon X is the last ATHX zone. In 2027, it's a **7-station circuit, scored on time, with a 25-minute cap**:

1. Rower (calories)
2. Dumbbell ground-to-overhead
3. Dumbbell bench press
4. Sandbag squats
5. Sandbag carry over 60 m
6. Burpees over a bench
7. Rower (calories)

> Reps and loads taken from the 2027 workouts published by the organiser: check them before your competition.

## Where the 2027 Metcon X is won

### The upper body

The overhead dumbbells then the **bench press** put two stations in a row that hammer the shoulders, chest and triceps. It's the big new feature of the season: very leg-dominant athletes are going to suffer. Technique in detail: [dumbbell ground-to-overhead](/athx-movements/dumbbell-ground-to-overhead) and [dumbbell bench press](/athx-movements/dumbbell-bench-press).

### The sandbag

Squats then a carry with the same bag: core and grip are put to the test. See [sandbag technique](/blog/sandbag-technique).

### The two rower stations

The rower opens and closes the workout. At the start, don't go out too fast; at the end, that's where places are won. See [rower technique](/athx-movements/rower).

## Race strategy

- **Break up your sets from the start**: for example 60 bench presses in sets of 12 to 15, with short rests, rather than going to failure.
- **Keep a steady pace on the opening row**: that's when adrenaline makes you go out too fast.
- **Train tired**: do simulations after a strength and running session, to reproduce real fatigue. More tips in [Metcon X explained](/blog/metcon-x-explained).

Since the loads differ by category, choose yours carefully: see [the ATHX categories](/athx-categories).

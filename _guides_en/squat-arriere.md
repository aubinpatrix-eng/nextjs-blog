## The standard

The back squat is tested as a **2RM** in the [2027 Strength zone](/athx-2027-workouts/strength-zone): two full reps with the same load. At the bottom, **the hip crease must go below the top of the knee**. At the top, hips and knees return to full extension.

## The technique step by step

1. **Set-up**: bar on the upper back (traps), narrow grip, elbows under the bar.
2. **Walk-out**: two small steps back, feet slightly wider than hips, toes turned out a little.
3. **Breathing**: big breath in, brace your core as if about to take a punch.
4. **Descent**: hips back and knees tracking over the feet, until you're below parallel.
5. **Drive up**: push the floor away, chest up, without the hips rising before the shoulders.

## Common mistakes

- **Not enough depth**: the number one cause of rejected reps.
- **Knees caving in** on the way up: strengthen the glutes and lighten the load.
- **Torso collapsing** forward: lack of bracing or ankle mobility.

## Progressing for the 2RM

- Train with percentages of your 1RM, up to sets of 2 to 3 reps between 85 and 92% in the final weeks.
- Add front squats and pause squats for position and strength at the bottom.
- Simulate the competition window: a timed build-up, then two attempts.

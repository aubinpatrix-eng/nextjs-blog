## The ATHX 2027 workouts at a glance

ATHX is made of **6 zones in a row over about 2h30**. Three are for preparing and recovering, three count towards the ranking.

| Zone | Duration | 2027 content | Score |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Warm-up zone | 30 min | Free warm-up | not scored |
| **Strength zone** | 20 min | 1RM shoulder-to-overhead, 2RM back squat, 3RM deadlift | total load |
| Refuel zone | 10 min | Hydration, refuelling | not scored |
| **Endurance zone** | 30 min | Fixed-distance run, then max SkiErg metres (24 min cap) | distance |
| Recovery zone | 30 min | Recovery | not scored |
| **Metcon X** | 30 min | Rower, dumbbells, bench press, sandbag, burpees (25 min cap) | time |

> The official workouts published by the organiser are the reference: read them again before your competition, details can change during the season.

## What changes from 2026

### An even more strength-focused Strength zone

In 2026, athletes tested a 1RM strict press, a 3RM squat and a 5RM deadlift. In 2027, it becomes **1RM, 2RM and 3RM**: fewer reps, heavier loads. And the strict press becomes a **shoulder-to-overhead**, where leg drive is allowed.

### A run-then-SkiErg Endurance zone

The run and rower combination is gone. In 2027, you run a **fixed distance (3 km in ATHX and Pro)**, then rack up **as many SkiErg metres as possible** in the time left, with a 24-minute cap. Pacing and the transition become decisive.

## Zone by zone

### Warm-up, Refuel and Recovery: the unscored zones

They don't earn any points, but they shape everything else. The **Warm-up zone** (30 min) gets you warm before the heavy loads. The **Refuel zone** (10 min) lets you drink and refuel before the endurance: see [what to eat in the Refuel zone](/blog/what-to-eat-refuel-zone). The **Recovery zone** (30 min) is there so you reach Metcon X fresh.

### Strength zone: pure strength

Three lifts, a score equal to the total load. All the details, time windows and attempt strategy in [the ATHX 2027 Strength zone](/athx-2027-workouts/strength-zone).

### Endurance zone: run then SkiErg

A fixed-distance run then a maximal effort on the SkiErg. Details and paces in [the ATHX 2027 Endurance zone](/athx-2027-workouts/endurance-zone).

### Metcon X: the finale

A 7-station circuit with a 25-minute cap, scored on time. Reps and loads per category in [the 2027 Metcon X](/athx-2027-workouts/metcon-x).

## How to prepare for the 2027 workouts

Each zone tests a different quality, so you need a program that trains heavy strength, endurance and conditioning **in parallel**. Allow **12 weeks** of specific preparation. Loads change depending on your category: see [the ATHX categories](/athx-categories).

| Category | Men | Women |

| Pro | 60 cal | 45 cal |

## The technique step by step

1. **Catch**: knees bent, shins vertical, arms straight, torso leaning slightly forward.
2. **Leg drive**: this does most of the work, arms still straight.
3. **Back swing**: the torso leans back slightly once the legs are straight.
4. **Arm pull**: the handle comes in below the chest.
5. **Recovery**: arms, then torso, then legs, in reverse order and without rushing.

## Race strategy

- **First row**: steady pace, heart rate under control. Adrenaline pushes you to go out too fast, and the rest of the workout pays for it.
- **Last row**: it's the final station, so go all out. Places are often decided here.

## Common mistakes

- **Pulling with the arms before driving with the legs.**
- **Stroke rate too high** with powerless strokes.
- **Feet badly strapped in**: take a few seconds to secure them properly.

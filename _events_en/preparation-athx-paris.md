## ATHX Paris 2027: the essentials

ATHX is back in Paris on **13 and 14 February 2027**, at the **Paris Event Center**, Porte de la Villette. It's the first French event of the 2027 season, and the venue of the very first Paris edition, in April 2026.

I was there: it was my first ATHX, and I finished **1st in the Strength zone** (I tell the whole story in [my ATHX Paris 2026 race report](/blog/athx-paris-2026-race-report)). This guide covers what I wish I'd known before going, and the plan I'd follow for February. Starting late? Follow our plan to [prepare for ATHX in 8 weeks](/blog/athx-8-week-training-plan). February coming round far too fast? The [ATHX Montpellier program](/athx-montpellier-training) starts on 8 March 2027, and [ATHX Marseille](/athx-marseille-training) in September leaves the whole summer to prepare.

## The events at ATHX Paris 2027

Paris follows the official 2027 season format:

| Zone | Duration | What's involved |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warm-up zone | 30 min | Warm-up and activation |
| **Strength zone** | 20 min | 1RM shoulder-to-overhead, 2RM back squat, 3RM deadlift |
| Refuel zone | 10 min | Hydration, refuelling |
| **Endurance zone** | 30 min | Run (3 km in ATHX and Pro), then as many SkiErg metres as possible, all within 24 min |
| Recovery zone | 30 min | Recovery |
| **Metcon X** | 30 min | Rower, dumbbells (ground-to-overhead and bench press), sandbag squats and carry, burpees, rower: 25 min cap |

Each zone is detailed in our guide to [the ATHX 2027 workouts](/athx-2027-workouts), and the loads per category in [the ATHX categories](/athx-categories).

Only the three zones in bold count towards the ranking. The Strength zone score is the **total load** lifted: every kilo counts. All the details in [the ATHX 2027 Strength zone explained](/athx-2027-workouts/strength-zone).

> The official workouts are the reference: read them again on the organiser's website before the competition.

## Your Paris training plan, week by week

Twelve weeks, from Monday 23 November 2026 to the competition:

| Weeks | Dates | Goal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 to 4 | 23 Nov → 20 Dec | Base: strength at 65-75%, aerobic endurance, SkiErg technique |
| 5 to 8 | 21 Dec → 17 Jan | Building load, first strength-then-cardio combinations |
| 9 to 11 | 18 Jan → 7 Feb | Heavy loads (85-92%), Metcon X simulations |
| 12 | 8 → 14 Feb | Deload and competition |

Want to try before committing? [Try the free ATHX program](/free-athx-training-week): a sample week of 5 sessions (strength and metcon on the SkiErg and rower), to download as a PDF.

### The Paris trap: the holidays mid-preparation

Christmas and New Year fall in **weeks 5 and 6**, just as the loads go up. Don't try to do everything: three short sessions a week are enough, as long as you keep **one heavy strength session** and **one endurance session**. Get back to a normal routine from 4 January.

### Training in winter

It's cold and dark: outdoor runs are harder to stick to. Replace part of your running with the SkiErg or rower indoors, and keep at least one long run a week so you don't lose the feel of the ground. On race day the hall is heated: train in light clothing indoors.

## My race-day tips for Paris

- **Arrive early**: the Paris Event Center is well served by metro and tram, but the check-in queues are long on Saturday morning.
- **Pick safe attempts** in the Strength zone: about 90% of your max for the first one. That's what let me make every lift. Estimate your loads with the [1RM calculator](/1rm-calculator).
- **Don't start too fast** in the Endurance zone: it comes right after strength and your legs are heavy. Read [managing the strength → endurance transition](/blog/strength-to-endurance-transition).
- **Prepare your refuelling** the day before: [what to eat in the Refuel zone](/blog/what-to-eat-refuel-zone).

If you're travelling from outside Paris, stay nearby the night before and read [what to eat the day before and the morning of ATHX](/blog/what-to-eat-before-athx).

## ATHX Montpellier 2027: the essentials

A newcomer to the French calendar, the **Montpellier** event takes place on **28 and 29 May 2027** at the **Parc des Expositions and Sud de France Arena** in Pérols. It's the ideal event if Paris in February came too soon: you have the whole winter to build your base, then 12 weeks of specific preparation in the spring. If you're already ready, [ATHX Paris training](/athx-paris-training) targets February; if May is too tight, see the [ATHX Marseille program](/athx-marseille-training) for September.

## The events at ATHX Montpellier 2027

The format is the 2027 season one, the same at every event:

| Zone | Duration | What's involved |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warm-up zone | 30 min | Warm-up and activation |
| **Strength zone** | 20 min | 1RM shoulder-to-overhead, 2RM back squat, 3RM deadlift |
| Refuel zone | 10 min | Hydration, refuelling |
| **Endurance zone** | 30 min | Run (3 km in ATHX and Pro), then as many SkiErg metres as possible, all within 24 min |
| Recovery zone | 30 min | Recovery |
| **Metcon X** | 30 min | Rower, dumbbells (ground-to-overhead and bench press), sandbag squats and carry, burpees, rower: 25 min cap |

Each zone is detailed in our guide to [the ATHX 2027 workouts](/athx-2027-workouts), and the loads per category in [the ATHX categories](/athx-categories).

The three zones in bold make up the ranking. To understand each one in detail: [What is ATHX Games?](/blog/what-is-athx-games)

## Your Montpellier training plan, week by week

| Weeks | Dates | Goal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 to 4 | 8 March → 4 April | Base: strength at 65-75%, aerobic endurance, technique |
| 5 to 8 | 5 April → 2 May | Building load, strength then SkiErg or run combinations |
| 9 to 11 | 3 → 23 May | Heavy loads (85-92%), Metcon X simulations |
| 12 | 24 → 29 May | Deload and competition |

Got some time before 8 March? Use it to try the [free ATHX program](/free-athx-training-week): a full week of 5 sessions to get a feel for the loads.

### The May bank holidays: protect your key sessions

The most intense phase (weeks 9 to 11) runs into **1 May, 8 May, Ascension Day (Thursday 6 May) and Whit Monday (17 May)**. That's when people go away for long weekends… and skip the sessions that matter. Put **heavy strength** and the **Metcon X simulation** early in the week, and keep the bank holidays for an endurance run.

### Preparing for the southern heat

At the end of May, temperatures can already climb around Montpellier. During the last three weeks, do **one endurance session a week in the hottest hours**, and get used to drinking during exercise. On race day, plan more water and sodium than usual: [what to eat in the Refuel zone](/blog/what-to-eat-refuel-zone).

## My race-day tips for Montpellier

- **Accommodation**: Pérols is close to the airport and the sea. Book early, the end of May is already holiday season.
- **Strength zone**: open at about 90% of your max, then go up. Estimate your 2RM and 3RM with the [1RM calculator](/1rm-calculator).
- **Endurance**: start carefully, the zone comes after strength. See [running and endurance: building an engine that lasts 2h30](/blog/running-rowing-athx-endurance).
- **Metcon X**: the sandbag often catches people out. Work on it from the first weeks with [sandbag technique](/blog/sandbag-technique).

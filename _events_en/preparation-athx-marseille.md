## ATHX Marseille 2027: the essentials

After a first edition in September 2026, ATHX returns to the **Parc Chanot** in Marseille on **10 and 11 September 2027**. It's the back-to-school event, with one particularity: **all your training happens in the summer**. Want an event earlier in the season? See [ATHX Paris](/athx-paris-training) in February or [ATHX Montpellier training](/athx-montpellier-training) for the end of May.

## The events at ATHX Marseille 2027

Marseille follows the 2027 season format:

| Zone | Duration | What's involved |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warm-up zone | 30 min | Warm-up and activation |
| **Strength zone** | 20 min | 1RM shoulder-to-overhead, 2RM back squat, 3RM deadlift |
| Refuel zone | 10 min | Hydration, refuelling |
| **Endurance zone** | 30 min | Run (3 km in ATHX and Pro), then as many SkiErg metres as possible, all within 24 min |
| Recovery zone | 30 min | Recovery |
| **Metcon X** | 30 min | Rower, dumbbells (ground-to-overhead and bench press), sandbag squats and carry, burpees, rower: 25 min cap |

Each zone is detailed in our guide to [the ATHX 2027 workouts](/athx-2027-workouts), and the loads per category in [the ATHX categories](/athx-categories).

Only Strength, Endurance and Metcon X count towards the ranking. Not sure about your category? Read [Lite, ATHX or Pro: which category to choose](/blog/choose-athx-category).

## Your Marseille training plan, week by week

| Weeks | Dates | Goal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 to 4 | 21 June → 18 July | Base: strength at 65-75%, aerobic endurance, technique |
| 5 to 8 | 19 July → 15 August | Building load, strength-then-cardio combinations |
| 9 to 11 | 16 August → 5 September | Heavy loads (85-92%), Metcon X simulations |
| 12 | 6 → 11 September | Deload and competition |

[Try the free ATHX program](/free-athx-training-week) before 21 June: 5 sessions to gauge your level and start your preparation with the right habits.

### A summer of training: the holiday strategy

Weeks 5 to 8 fall right in July and August. If you go away, take a **minimal plan of 3 sessions a week**: strength if you can find a gym, endurance (running, cycling, swimming) and a bodyweight circuit. Never stop for more than a week. Weeks 9 to 11, from 16 August, need to be done in the gym: that's where the heavy loads are built.

### Dealing with the heat

July and August in Marseille often mean more than 30 °C. Train **early in the morning or in the evening**, drink before you're thirsty, and add sodium on long sessions. Slightly lower your endurance paces during heatwaves: perceived effort matters more than the clock. For hydration on race day: [what to eat in the Refuel zone](/blog/what-to-eat-refuel-zone).

## My race-day tips for Marseille

- **Back to school** falls in competition week in France: plan ahead (work, kids) so your deload week is really restful.
- **Strength zone**: open carefully, then go up. My attempt guidelines are in [the ATHX 2027 Strength zone explained](/athx-2027-workouts/strength-zone).
- **In a pair?** Marseille is a friendly event: do the endurance and Metcon X sessions together, it's easier to stick with them all summer. See [training for ATHX as a pair](/blog/athx-doubles-training).
- **Metcon X**: practise linking movements while tired. See [Metcon X explained](/blog/metcon-x-explained).

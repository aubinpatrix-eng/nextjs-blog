## Data collected

- **Purchase**: email, declared PRs (squat, overhead press, deadlift) and target category, sent to Stripe at payment to prepare your program. Card details are processed by Stripe only.
- **Newsletter** (if you sign up): your email address, managed by our email provider Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France). You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in every email.

The PRs entered in the home page calculator stay in your browser until you click the buy button.

## Purposes and retention

Your data is used to send you your program and, if you asked for it, the newsletter. It is kept for 12 months, and billing data for the legally required period.

## Your rights

You can request access to, correction or deletion of your data by writing to **aubinpatrix@gmail.com**. You can also lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority (cnil.fr).

## Cookies

This website does not use advertising cookies.

We use Google Analytics (Google Ireland Ltd.) to measure the site's audience: page views, visit duration, where visitors come from. This data is statistical and is not used for advertising. These cookies are only set with your consent, given through the banner shown on your first visit. Until you accept, Google Analytics sets no cookies. You can change your mind at any time with the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page.
